Quote to job card

When the customer says yes, the worker turns the quote into a job request. The owner sees it in Jobs, and Claude enters it into the workshop system.

Concept only. Names, plates and numbers are examples. Nothing is saved or sent.
  1. Quote: new green button Start job next to Send.
  2. Job form: take a photo of the registration card, add phone and km. Oil, filter and labour come from the quote.
  3. Sent: the request waits for the owner.
  4. Jobs (owner): waiting, entered with its job card number, or a part that was missing.
  5. Print sticker: after the job is sent, print the next-service sticker for the windscreen on the Brother printer.
  6. New device: a new phone or iPad can see customer data only after the owner approves it.

Service sticker templates

Shown close to real size, black only for the Brother QL-820NWB. A, B and C fit the DK-22113 clear 62 mm roll (it is continuous, so any length). D needs a 29 mm roll. Values are examples: next date is 6 months after the visit, next km adds 5,000 to today's km until you set the real interval for each oil.
